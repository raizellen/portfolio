My story

Most of my work so far has been helping other people understand things. Follow the path from the start.

Apr 2019 to Dec 2020

Academic assistant

North Shore Coaching College, Norwest

My first job. I answered students' questions, explained concepts at each student's level, wrote feedback on their writing and homework, and handled admin.

Aug 2023 to Nov 2023

Crew member

McDonald's, Schofields

A crash course in speed and teamwork. I kept service quick and consistent in a high-volume store and worked with the team to decide what came first when things got busy.

Dec 2023 to Nov 2026

Coding coach

Skill Samurai, Rouse Hill

I taught students aged 6 to 14 to code in Java, Python, Scratch and Lua, and keep the sessions fun and on track. When a project breaks, I walked through the error step by step so the student finds the fix themselves. I also planned lessons, tracked each student's progress, and kept parents updated.

Feb 2025 to June 2025

U:PASS leader

University of Technology Sydney

I ran weekly peer-assisted study sessions for Programming 2. I wrote session plans and practice material on Java and Python, and guided students through debugging and design problems by asking questions instead of handing over answers.

Now

Final year

UTS

Currently building StyleU with a team of seven, as well as an adaptive heads-up display for my capstone. Come and see them in the project gallery!
